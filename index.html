Fluentry Learn Hub

Interactive, research-informed digital applications designed to accelerate Japanese and East Asian language acquisition through computational linguistics, corpus analysis, and memory algorithms.

🎓 Academic Research & Pedagogical Foundation

Each tool in the Learn Hub is developed directly from published empirical research in Computer-Assisted Language Learning (CALL). By combining computational modeling, error analysis of second-language learners, and adaptive algorithms, these applications turn complex linguistic structures into intuitive digital practice.

Live Tool

Mini Doushi

ミニ動詞 ・ Japanese Conjugation Trainer

A systematic drill application for mastering Japanese verb and adjective inflections (A1–A2 levels). Designed to build rapid, accurate morphosyntactic recall under classroom and self-study conditions.

Research Base: Built on error-analysis data collected from university learners to isolate and address common conjugation stumbling blocks (Librenjak et al., MIPRO / CALL).

Kurutta Kotoba

狂った言葉 ・ Kanji & Word Puzzle

A fast-paced interactive puzzle game where learners connect kanji, hiragana, and morphological roots under time constraints to reinforce intuitive word formation and vocabulary recognition.

Research Base: Integrates native-language mnemonic association and spaced repetition principles (Librenjak, Digital Resources for Learning Japanese).

In Development

Kanji Sorter

漢字ソーター ・ Expert System & Learning Optimizer

An adaptive optimization tool that calculates individual character "learning cost" by analyzing stroke complexity, component decomposition, frequency across media, and semantic clusters.

Research Base: Based on PhD research introducing an expert system using topological sorting algorithms to determine the most efficient kanji learning sequence (Librenjak, Silva Iaponicarum & EAJS).
